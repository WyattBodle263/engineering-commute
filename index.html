About the show

Hi, I'm Wyatt. I started The Engineering Commute because the drive to work is the perfect time to learn something, and most engineering explainers are built for people staring at diagrams, not people watching the road.

So every episode is made to be heard. One clear picture to hold onto, a story or two, and a quick pit stop halfway through so nobody gets lost. If you've ever wondered how something works and never had time to look it up, this one's for you.
